Wholesale Inquiries

Partner with Firecraft

Bring the heat to your shelves! We partner with independent retailers, specialty food stores, and restaurants nationwide. Our wholesale program offers competitive pricing, fast shipping, and robust marketing support.

Wholesale Requirements

  • Minimum Order Quantity: 4 cases (48 bottles total) per order.
  • Documentation: You must provide a valid Tax ID and a Reseller Certificate.
  • Lead Time: Most wholesale orders are fulfilled and shipped within 3-5 business days.
  • Payment Terms: Initial orders require payment upfront. Net-30 terms are available for established partners after 3 successful orders.
  • Pricing Integrity: Partners must agree to our MAP (Minimum Advertised Price) policy to maintain brand value.

How to Apply

Send an email to wholesale@firecraft.com with your business name, location, and a brief description of your store or restaurant. Our wholesale team will review your application and get back to you within 2 business days with our complete product catalog and pricing sheet.

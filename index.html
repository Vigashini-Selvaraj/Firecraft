FLAVOR DISCOVERY

Discover By Flavor.

Not all heat is created equal. We carefully craft distinct flavor profiles to match every culinary need—from sweet and fruity glazes to deeply roasted, savory barbecue blends.

View All Flavors

Sweet Heat

Pineapple, Mango, Agave

Experience a delicate balance of natural fruit sugars and fiery chili heat. Perfect for glazing wings, adding to stir-fries, or mixing into marinades.

Smoky & Rich

Mesquite, Chipotle, Bourbon

Deep, rich, and intensely savory. Achieved by slow-roasting peppers over open wood fires. Ideal for BBQ, steaks, and hearty stews.

Roasted Garlic

Black Garlic, Shallots, Thyme

A pungent, aromatic profile that pairs beautifully with almost any dish. We use roasted garlic to bring out a mellow, buttery warmth.

Fruity Burst

Papaya, Guava, Habanero

Bright, vibrant, and tropical. Featuring real chunks of tropical fruit and habanero, these sauces bring a sunny, island-inspired kick.

Tangy & Sharp

Apple Cider, Mustard, Lime

A sharp, acidic bite driven by apple cider vinegar and citrus juices. This profile cuts through rich foods perfectly, making it the ultimate pairing for pulled pork.

Zesty Citrus

Lemon, Yuzu, Jalapeño

Zesty and refreshing. Infused with lime and lemon zest to awaken the palate before the heat kicks in. Amazing on seafood, tacos, and fresh salads.

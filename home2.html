PAIRING GUIDE

The right sauce changes everything.

Not sure which style belongs on your plate? Start with the food and we'll point you in the right direction.

FLAVOR DIRECTION

Smoky · Tangy

WHY IT WORKS

Adds depth without overpowering the meat. The perfect combination of tanginess and deep smoke complements grilled and fried chicken beautifully.

Enhances the overall dining experience by adding an unforgettable layer of complexity to your meals.

FLAVOR DIRECTION

Garlic · Smoky

WHY IT WORKS

Cuts through rich, savory flavors. A bold, garlicky profile elevates the heavy umami flavors of a good burger.

Enhances the overall dining experience by adding an unforgettable layer of complexity to your meals.

FLAVOR DIRECTION

Fruity · Tangy

WHY IT WORKS

Provides a bright, acidic contrast to fatty meats and heavy cheeses, while adding the perfect kick of heat.

Enhances the overall dining experience by adding an unforgettable layer of complexity to your meals.

FLAVOR DIRECTION

Sweet · Heat

WHY IT WORKS

Sweetness cuts through the saltiness of cured meats like pepperoni, while the chili adds a lingering warmth.

Enhances the overall dining experience by adding an unforgettable layer of complexity to your meals.

BEHIND THE BRAND

A simple idea became a brand.

Fireborn started with a simple question: what if hot sauce could be something you reached for because of the flavor, not just because you wanted more heat?

That idea became our starting point. Today, we're continuing to explore the relationship between chili, food and flavor.

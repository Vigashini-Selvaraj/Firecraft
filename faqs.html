Frequently Asked Questions

Do your sauces contain artificial preservatives?

No, we use only natural preservatives like vinegar, salt, and citrus juices. We believe in keeping our ingredients as clean and natural as possible.

Are Firecraft sauces gluten-free and vegan?

Yes! All of our core hot sauces are 100% gluten-free and certified vegan. They are made entirely from plant-based ingredients.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we only ship within the United States and Canada. We hope to expand our shipping zones globally in the near future!

How long does a bottle last after opening?

We recommend refrigerating our sauces after opening. Once refrigerated, a bottle will easily stay fresh and retain its heat for up to 6 months.

Which sauce is the hottest?

The "Ghost Pepper Reserve" is currently our hottest offering, clocking in at over 500,000 Scoville Heat Units. Proceed with caution!

What happens if my bottle arrives broken?

If your package arrives damaged, please email us a photo within 48 hours of delivery at support@firecraft.com, and we will ship a free replacement immediately.

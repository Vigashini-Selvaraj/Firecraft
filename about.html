THE BEGINNING

It started with a
single pepper.

Years ago, our founder stood in a cramped kitchen staring at a basket of habaneros, frustrated by the hot sauces on the market. They were either face-meltingly hot with no depth, or flavorful but lacking any real punch.

The mission became clear: bridge the gap. We wanted a sauce that respected the complexity of the chili pepper—highlighting its natural fruity notes, earthy undertones, and vibrant color—before delivering that satisfying burn.

Hundreds of batches later, the Fireborn standard was set.

Driving the

The Vision

To redefine the hot sauce industry globally. We want to prove that extreme heat and exquisite, gourmet flavor can coexist perfectly in every single drop, pushing culinary boundaries to new heights.

The Mission

To source the finest, most vibrant ingredients from sustainable farmers, and meticulously craft small-batch sauces that elevate everyday meals into unforgettable experiences. No compromises.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

More than just heat.

We refuse to compromise on our core tenets. This is how we ensure every bottle is perfect.

Flavor First

We build our recipes around flavor profiles first, only introducing heat to elevate the ingredients, never to mask them.

Small Batch

Mass production kills character. We cook in small, controlled batches to maintain the artisanal quality of our sauces.

Real Ingredients

No artificial extracts. No fillers. Just real, fresh peppers, aromatics, vinegars, and spices sourced responsibly.
